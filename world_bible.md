Game Title: VOID WARRANTY
1. The World: The United Corporate Stewardship (UCS)
The Vibe: High-tech feudalism. The sky is full of neon ads, but the streets are full of trash.

Power Structure:

The Board: The unseen rulers.

The Algorithms: The laws. Decisions (who lives, who dies, who gets a loan) are made by "Risk Assessment AIs." They are never wrong, because they define what is "correct."

Governments: Reduced to "Customer Service" departments for the Corps. They handle the complaints so the Corps don't have to.

Religion: "The Grand Ledger." It’s not a church; it’s a cultural mindset. Morality is transactional. Being poor is a sin because it means you are "inefficient." People pray to the "RNG" (Random Number Generator) to grant their insurance claims.

Everyday Life:

Subscription Living: You don't buy a toaster; you subscribe to "Heat Services." If you miss a payment, the toaster bricks itself.

The Dopamine Trap: Life is miserable, but "Sensory Sims" (VR addiction), synthetic alcohol, and gambling are cheap. Rebellion is hard when you’re sedated.

2. The Characters
The Protagonist: Alex (The Pilot)

Age: 19.

Background: A vocational student studying "Remote Asset Management" (Drone Piloting). He is good at it because he grew up playing cracked versions of combat sims.

Motivation: Sophie. He doesn't care about politics or the world. He just wants to pay for his sister's "Subscription-Tier 1" meds.

The Family

Tonya (Mom): 45, but looks 60. Former factory worker, injured on the job, claim denied ("Operator Error"). Now works 3 "Gig" jobs (cleaning bots, data entry). Self-medicates with cheap synthetic gin to sleep.

Sophie (Sister): 10. Has "Filter-Lung" (a common disease from breathing recycled air in low-income housing). The cure exists, but it’s "Out of Network."

The Mentor/Fixer: Varrick

Role: CEO of "Second Chance Logistics" (a Chop Shop).

Vibe: He isn't a hero. He wears a cheap suit. He smokes real tobacco (a status symbol).

Philosophy: "The Corp isn't evil, kid. It's a shark. It eats. I'm a remora. I eat the scraps. You want to survive? Stop trying to be the ocean."

The Antagonist: Senior Risk Officer Silas

Role: A mid-level bureaucrat for the UCS.

Vibe: He never raises his voice. He is terrifyingly polite. He views Alex not as an enemy, but as a "Statistical Anomaly" that needs to be corrected.

3. The Story Spine & Progression
Act 1: The Fine Print (The Setup)
The Hook: Alex lands a dream job: Salvage Technician for a subsidiary of UCS. "Competitive Pay! Full Benefits!"

The Reality: The job is dangerous. He pilots a remote "Loader" (The Jeep) to strip valuable parts from wrecked ships.

The Turning Point: Sophie takes a turn for the worse. Alex works double shifts. On a mission, he finds a Black Box (Data Core) in a wreck that shouldn't be there.

The Data: It proves the UCS is deliberately venting toxic waste into the poor sectors to save on disposal fees. This is what caused Sophie’s illness.

The Fall: Alex tries to report it via the company "Whistleblower Hotline."

The Trap: The hotline is automated. It flags him as a "Liability."

The Consequences: Instantly, his bank account is locked ("Suspicious Activity"). His Mom’s rent is tripled ("Market Adjustment"). His Citizenship Score drops to "Prohibited." He is fired for "Theft of Company Property" (The Black Box).

Act 2: The Void (The Gameplay Loop)
The Pivot: Desperate and blacklisted, Alex takes his stolen salvage codes to Varrick.

The Deal: Varrick agrees to source "Black Market Insulin" for Sophie. In exchange, Alex must use his piloting skills to rob Corporate ships.

The Gameplay:

Boarding: Alex flies Varrick’s rust-bucket ship.

Deploying: He deploys the "Units" (Jeep/Tank/Heli) to steal goods.

The Progression: Alex realizes he can't just steal meds; he needs to break the system. He starts stealing AI Processor Nodes to build a transmitter powerful enough to override the Corporate Media Blockade.

The Revelation: Alex builds "The Broadcast." He intends to stream the Black Box data to every screen in the sector, proving the Corp is poisoning the air.

Act 3: The Settlement (The Climax)
The Raid: Alex launches a suicide mission on the UCS Regional Server Hub. He has to physically drive the "Jeep" (carrying the Broadcast Core) to the main antenna array.

The Confrontation: Just as he is about to plug it in, Officer Silas hacks his comms. He doesn't send soldiers; he sends a contract.

The Offer: "Alex. We have authorized a 'Compassionate Care Grant' for Sophie. Full treatment. A new lung. Paid for. We will also clear your debt and offer you a Senior position in Logistics. All you have to do... is throw that Core off the ledge."

4. The Ending: The Cynical Cycle
Choice A: The Martyr (The "Good" Ending?)

Action: Alex screams "No!" and plugs in the Core.

Result: The Broadcast goes out. The sector riots. The Corp stock dips 4%.

Fate: Alex’s ship is blown out of the sky by a drone. He dies.

The Aftermath: Sophie gets her meds—not because the Corp is nice, but because the PR department issues a "Settlement Payout" to victims of the "Toxic Venting Scandal" to calm the riots. The system survives, but Sophie lives.

Choice B: The Employee (The "Bad" Ending)

Action: Alex looks at a photo of Sophie. He drops the Core.

Result: He is extracted. Sophie is cured. They move to a nice apartment.

Fate: Fast forward 10 years. Alex is now wearing a suit like Silas. He looks tired. He is drinking like his mom used to.
