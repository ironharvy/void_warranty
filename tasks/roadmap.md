# Roadmap

## Milestone 0: Pre-Production Lock

Deliverables:
- Game pillars approved.
- One-page GDD approved.
- Vertical slice approved.
- First vehicle selected.
- Engine choice locked to Unity.

Immediate tasks:
- Create Unity project.
- Implement test scene.
- Build temporary high-angle camera.
- Prototype Runner controls.

## Milestone 1: Core Prototype

Goal:
Make a graybox scene where the player can drive the Runner through a simple interior, collect an objective, and reach extraction.

Deliverables:
- Vehicle movement.
- Camera follow and aim readability.
- Objective pickup.
- Damage or hazard prototype.
- Success and failure states.

## Milestone 2: Slice Mission Flow

Goal:
Turn the graybox into a complete black box retrieval mission.

Deliverables:
- Mission briefing UI.
- Scripted insertion.
- Interior route with one meaningful obstacle.
- Alarm or escalation state after pickup.
- Post-mission result screen.

## Milestone 3: Slice Presentation

Goal:
Give the mission enough tone and feedback to evaluate the final direction.

Deliverables:
- Temporary stylized materials.
- Sound cues and music placeholder.
- Comms dialogue.
- Corporate signage and ad dressing.
- Basic Varrick scene or debrief wrapper.

## Milestone 4: Expansion Decision

Goal:
Decide whether to scale up after the vertical slice is genuinely fun.

Decision criteria:
- Is the vehicle handling compelling?
- Does extraction create tension?
- Is the world legible from the camera?
- Can content be expanded through reusable mission patterns?

## First 10 Concrete Tasks

1. Create a Unity project and commit the empty baseline.
2. Set up folders for scenes, scripts, prefabs, scriptable objects, UI, and audio.
3. Build a test arena with walls, ramps, a pickup, and an extraction zone.
4. Implement Runner controller with acceleration, turning, braking, and boost placeholder.
5. Implement high-angle camera tuned for readability over spectacle.
6. Add objective pickup and mission success trigger.
7. Add one environmental hazard, such as electrified floor or turret fire.
8. Add alarm state that starts a countdown after objective pickup.
9. Add temporary mission briefing and post-mission UI.
10. Playtest and tune handling before adding more content.
