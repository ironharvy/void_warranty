# One-Page GDD

## High Concept

VOID WARRANTY is a 3D single-player vehicle action game for PC built in Unity. In a dystopian corporate future, the player boards derelict and hostile ships and deploys specialized vehicles to retrieve black-market tech, evidence, and survival-critical cargo.

## Player Fantasy

Control compact futuristic vehicles inside dangerous ship interiors, secure high-value objectives, and escape while the ship's defenses escalate.

## Core Loop

1. Receive mission briefing and target objective.
2. Board or breach target ship through a scripted insertion.
3. Choose or deploy a vehicle suited to the task.
4. Traverse the ship interior while avoiding hazards and defenses.
5. Recover the target item.
6. Survive the extraction phase as danger ramps up.
7. Return to base for upgrades, payoffs, and story progress.

## Core Camera And Controls

- High-angle tactical action camera inspired by Return Fire.
- Player directly controls only deployable vehicles.
- Mothership movement is off-screen and used only for narrative framing.

## Vehicle Roles

### Runner
- Fast and fragile.
- Best for objective grabs and narrow routes.
- Weak in direct combat.

### Breaker
- Slow and durable.
- Can smash through armored barriers and absorb turret fire.
- Useful for high-risk extraction paths.

### Skimmer
- Hover-capable utility vehicle.
- Crosses gaps and handles vertical or unstable spaces.
- Lower durability than Breaker, more mobility than Runner.

## Mission Structure

Each mission should contain:
- An insertion point.
- A readable interior route network.
- One primary objective.
- Optional salvage pickups.
- At least one gate that favors a specific vehicle role.
- A post-objective escalation event.
- A clear extraction zone.

## Enemies And Hazards

Initial slice should stay small:
- Stationary turrets.
- Patrol drones.
- Locked doors or armored shutters.
- Electrical floors, coolant leaks, reactor instability, or vacuum breaches.

## Progression

Progression should focus on utility and survivability, not large RPG systems.

Upgrade examples:
- Better armor.
- Faster extraction speed.
- Cargo handling improvements.
- ECM or jammer module.
- Limited-use repair charge.

## Narrative Delivery

Story is delivered through:
- Mission briefings.
- Comms during play.
- Short base scenes.
- Environmental text and corporate signage.

## Win Condition

Retrieve the mission objective and extract.

## Failure Conditions

- Vehicle destroyed.
- Objective lost or unrecoverable.
- Extraction timer expires.
- Critical mission hazard overwhelms the route.
