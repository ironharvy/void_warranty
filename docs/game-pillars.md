# Game Pillars

## Project Summary

VOID WARRANTY is a solo-developed 3D action game in Unity with a presentation style inspired by Return Fire. The player does not fly the mothership directly. Missions begin once Alex's crew has already breached or boarded a target ship, and the player controls specialized vehicles sent inside to recover objectives and escape before the situation collapses.

## Pillar 1: Vehicle-First Boarding Fantasy

The core fantasy is not "be a soldier" or "be a captain." It is "deploy the right machine into a hostile ship, improvise under pressure, and get out with the prize."

This means:
- The player always controls a vehicle, not an on-foot character.
- Each vehicle has a distinct role and level of access.
- Interior spaces are designed around traversal, hazard management, and objective retrieval.

## Pillar 2: Fast, Readable Tactical Action

The camera should feel close to Return Fire: high-angle, readable, and focused on quick decision-making instead of simulation detail.

This means:
- Controls must be simple and responsive.
- Combat and hazards need to read clearly from a distance.
- Mission spaces should emphasize route choice, not maze complexity.

## Pillar 3: Extraction Pressure

Every mission should feel like a theft under a tightening clock. The tension spikes when the objective is secured and escape becomes harder than entry.

This means:
- The mission state changes after the objective is taken.
- Reinforcements, alarms, or ship instability increase pressure during extraction.
- Winning is about getting out, not clearing the map.

## Pillar 4: Corporate Decay Atmosphere

The world should feel like industrial neglect wrapped in premium branding. The tone is cynical, commercial, and hostile without becoming visually noisy or narratively overwritten.

This means:
- Ships feel like products, not just levels.
- UI, signage, ads, and mission text reinforce the corporate setting.
- Story delivery stays efficient: comms chatter, mission briefings, ads, and short scene framing instead of expensive cutscenes.

## Pillar 5: Solo-Buildable Scope

The game must be designed around what one developer can finish with AI assistance and modular asset sourcing.

This means:
- Small number of vehicle classes.
- Reusable mission structure.
- Limited enemy set.
- Strong use of placeholder content early.
- Vertical slice must prove the game before broad content expansion.

## Non-Goals

The project is not trying to be:
- A fully simulated space combat game.
- An open-world RPG.
- A stealth sandbox with deep NPC behavior.
- A large branching narrative production with cinematic cutscenes.
- A fully bespoke art pipeline from day one.
